**Gold:** p.213 Figure C1 header, under IDENTIFIED RESOURCES > Demonstrated  
**Queries:** ['Figure C1 mineral-resource classification diagram Demonstrated', 'Demonstrated subdivided categories Figure C1']  views=[]  
**Answer:** In the mineral-resource classification diagram (Figure C1), the 'Demonstrated' (identified resources) category is subdivided into two categories: "Measured" and "Indicated." Measured resources have quantity computed from closely spaced sampling and measurements, establishing size, shape, depth, and mineral content well. Indicated resources have quantity and grade computed from less closely spaced sampling, with a lower degree of assurance but enough to assume continuity between observation points. There is also an "Inferred" category based on assumed continuity beyond measured and indicated resources, but it is not part of the 'Demonstrated' subdivision. Thus, 'Demonstrated' resources are subdivided into "Measured" and "Indicated." [p212-t1, p211-t8, p213-t1]
